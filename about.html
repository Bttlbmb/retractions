Sources & checks

How cases are built

Each case follows one public publication and connects its claim to a specific study and a formal correction or retraction. Separate publications using the same research have separate case pages linked to each other. Source links, document pages and publication dates are provided on the evidence page.

Cases were researched and checked independently by separate agents, then reviewed together. Each evidence page gives the source check date, publication date and date of the research change.

Major, moderate and minor changes

Each case, meaning one public source that cites one study, is marked Major, Moderate or Minor. This is an editorial assessment of how much the research change matters to the claim the source makes.

MajorThe claim no longer stands or is badly off. The larger of the two figures is at least 50% higher than the smaller and the conclusion changes. Or the claim is reversed, removed or crosses a threshold it relies on. Or a retracted study was the main support and nothing close replaces it.

ModerateThe claim is partly affected. The larger figure is 10% to 50% higher than the smaller, or more but the conclusion holds. Or a retracted study was one of several supports, or its replacement result is close. Or only a description was corrected.

MinorThe larger figure is less than 10% higher than the smaller, or the change stays within the stated uncertainty, and the conclusion is unchanged.

How severity was checked: two reviewers, both AI agents working from the case records rather than the original sources, classified every case without seeing each other’s work. They agreed on 99 of 106 cases. They settled 6 of the 7 disagreements in a second round; the editor settled the last.

Severity is a judgement about the claim as the source states it. It is not a verdict on the research or on the publisher.

Starting points

The search used Retraction Watch, its public dataset via Crossref, publisher notices and the public pages that reuse the findings. German-speaking countries, the UK and recent changes were prioritised.
