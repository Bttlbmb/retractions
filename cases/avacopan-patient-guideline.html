A patient guideline retains an avacopan recommendation after its pivotal trial was retracted

German rheumatology society · DGRh

Why it matters

The brochure supports patient–doctor treatment decisions. DGRh warned separately in July, but that warning is absent from the current guideline catalogue entry and patient download.

Still says

Patient version 1.0 · currently offered for download
Diese Empfehlung ist neu und basiert auf Daten aus der ADVOCATE-Studie.

Page 13 · explanation of recommendation 5.4 on page 12 · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe patient guide recommends considering avacopan to reduce steroid exposure and explicitly bases the recommendation on ADVOCATE, reporting better remission and fewer steroid-related side effects.

What changed

Retracted29 Jun 2026

NEJM retracted ADVOCATE after undisclosed endpoint reassessment following unblinding. EU authorisation was revoked on 4 August 2026 after regulators found efficacy was no longer demonstrated.

The recommendation is optional and predates the retraction. DGRh’s separate July statement advises against new prescribing. This case concerns the unchanged patient resource still supplied through the guideline catalogue, rather than a new society endorsement after the withdrawal.

How the claim connects to the study

The patient brochure explicitly names ADVOCATE as the evidentiary basis of recommendation 5.4 and describes its comparison of avacopan with glucocorticoids. The original NEJM trial and formal notice identify the same named study. The society’s current catalogue directly supplies the 26-page patient brochure.

    The trial data could no longer demonstrate efficacy; supportive evidence was insufficient. The EU authorisation was revoked.
