A wealth-tax commentary uses Austria’s original poverty estimate after its correction

Unsere Zeitung

Why it matters

The commentary uses the old estimate to argue for taxes on wealth and stronger redistribution.

Still says

13 June 2026
Das entspricht 18,8 Prozent der Gesamtbevölkerung

Comparison of millionaires and poverty risk · Open the source ↗

In EnglishUnsere Zeitung contrasts the number of dollar millionaires with an estimated 1.69 million people, or 18.8%, at risk of poverty or social exclusion in Austria.

What changed

Corrected12 May 2026

Statistics Austria corrected a data-linkage error and recalculated indicators based on household income. Its May 2026 revision put the estimate at 18.6%, or 1,686,000 people.

The article rounds the population count to 1.69 million and retains the original 18.8%. Its comparison with the number of millionaires is the author’s political argument. The survey correction is within its sampling interval.

How the claim connects to the study
