A business magazine cites a withdrawn climate-damage estimate in adaptation guidance

forum Nachhaltig Wirtschaften · signed magazine article

Why it matters

A DIN climate-project coordinator presents the figure in public guidance about business resilience, regulatory requirements and standards.

Still says

1 March 2026
verursachen die Folgen des Klimawandels weltweit jährlich Schäden von 38 Billionen US-Dollar.

Opening study paragraph · Klimaresilient wirtschaften · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe magazine article reports annual worldwide climate damage of US$38 trillion and uses the figure to argue that businesses should adapt and apply climate-related standards.

What changed

Retracted3 Dec 2025

Nature retracted the underlying paper after data and modelling problems required revisions too extensive for a correction.

This is a signed magazine contribution. The underlying study projected annual damage by 2050. The article states an annual figure without that date. Other discussion of standards and legal requirements draws on separate material.

How the claim connects to the study

The article names the Potsdam Institute for Climate Impact Research and the exact title The economic commitment of climate change. Its US$38 trillion figure matches the withdrawn paper. The current article contains no retraction note.
