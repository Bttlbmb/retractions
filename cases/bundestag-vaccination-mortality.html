A parliamentary witness statement still cites a retracted mortality paper

Helmut Sterz · Bundestag hearing submission

Why it matters

The document remains available as evidence submitted to the Bundestag’s inquiry into the pandemic response and lessons for future vaccination policy.

Still says

Statement dated 16 March 2026 · still distributed
Das Resultat der Massenvakzinierung … eine deutliche Übersterblichkeit

PDF page 9 / printed page 8 · claim and footnote 6 · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe witness statement attributes excess mortality in Western countries to mass vaccination and cites Mostert et al. at that claim.

What changed

Retracted11 Aug 2026

BMJ retracted the paper over misinformation about possible mortality causes and limited original work. The study did not establish vaccination as a cause of excess deaths.

Limits and context

This is one witness’s submission, not a Bundestag finding. The hearing report prominently includes Karl Lauterbach’s rebuttal. The missing update is the cited paper’s later retraction. The withdrawal concerns causal discussion and limited original analysis, not fabricated mortality counts or a finding that excess deaths did not occur.

How the claim connects to the study

Footnote 6 identifies Mostert et al., the full title and BMJ article number e000282. Its DOI has an extra hyphen, but the paper’s identity is unambiguous. The current hearing page directly offers the nine-page statement. It predates the August retraction and contains no later status note.
