Half of adults now live with a long-term health condition · Open the source ↗

NatCen’s launch release reports 32% for mobility impairment, 28% for stamina, breathing or fatigue and 26% for mental health impairment among Scottish adults with a long-term condition.

What changed

The Scottish Government corrected Table 2.4 and its accompanying text in March 2026. The revised estimates for the same three categories are 41%, 37% and 32%.

The percentages refer to adults who report a long-term condition. Respondents could report more than one kind of impairment. The correction applies to individual categories. The separate 50% headline is the share of all adults with a long-term condition.

Why it matters

The release presents the survey’s findings to the public and media, including the types of impairment reported by adults with long-term conditions.

How the claim connects to the study

The press release identifies the 2024 Scottish Health Survey and its producer ScotCen. The government correction covers the same original category estimates in Table 2.4 and its summary.
