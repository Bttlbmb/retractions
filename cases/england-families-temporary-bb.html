The National Plan to End Homelessness retains the original B&B count

UK Government · National Plan to End Homelessness

Why it matters

The national plan uses the figure to describe the problem it aims to address and explain its commitment to end unlawful B&B use for families.

Still says

11 December 2025 · national plan
2,070 families had been living in this type of accommodation for longer than the 6-week statutory limit.

Section 1.1 · footnote 17 · Open the source ↗

The National Plan to End Homelessness gives 2,070 families in England staying in bed and breakfast accommodation beyond six weeks in June 2025.

What changed

Corrected26 Feb 2026

The housing ministry corrected a recording problem in February 2026. The earlier count did not properly add together multiple short B&B placements when applying the six-week limit. The revised count for 30 June 2025 is 2,130.

The corrected series is an England-wide snapshot for households with children or a pregnant member. The 3,340 households in B&B accommodation of any duration in June 2025 are counted separately.
