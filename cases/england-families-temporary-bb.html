United Kingdom·Social policy

Two UK strategies retain a corrected count of families in long B&B stays

UK Government · National Plan to End Homelessness·11 December 2025 · national plan

Corrected26 Feb 2026

Still says

2,070 families had been living in this type of accommodation for longer than the 6-week statutory limit.

Section 1.1 · footnote 17 · Open the source ↗

The National Plan to End Homelessness and the Child Poverty Strategy give 2,070 families in England staying in bed and breakfast accommodation beyond six weeks in June 2025.

What changed

The housing ministry corrected a recording problem in February 2026. The earlier count did not properly add together multiple short B&B placements when applying the six-week limit. The revised count for 30 June 2025 is 2,130.

The corrected series is an England-wide snapshot for households with children or a pregnant member. The 3,340 households in B&B accommodation of any duration in June 2025 are counted separately.

Why it matters

Both strategies use the figure to describe the problem they are trying to address and to explain commitments on temporary accommodation.

How the claim connects to the study

The national plan cites the original quarterly release at footnote 17. The Child Poverty Strategy links the same official table series at footnote 130. The ministry’s notice identifies the affected series, and table TA1 gives the corrected count.
