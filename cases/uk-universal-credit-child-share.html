United Kingdom·Social policy

A policy digest still says more than half of children will be in Universal Credit households

Smart Thinking · Resolution Foundation report·29 January 2026

CorrectedCorrection date unclear

Still says

half of all children (54 per cent) in Britain today.

Report summary · second paragraph · Open the source ↗

Smart Thinking’s report summary says 54% of children will live in households receiving Universal Credit after rollout. It links readers to the Resolution Foundation study.

What changed

The corrected report lowers that share from 54% to 42%. Its correction notice explicitly identifies the original figure as wrong; the other figures and analysis are unchanged.

The 6.5 million child count was not changed. The publisher’s main report page now uses 42%; its press release is only partly updated, retaining “half” in its opening and “more than half” in a quotation beside the corrected percentage. The notice prints 4 February 2025 despite the report being dated January 2026, so the correction’s exact date is left unresolved.

Why it matters

The error changes the description from a minority to a majority of children in a public summary of proposals to reform the UK benefits system.

How the claim connects to the study

The digest names the report’s title, four authors, publisher and date, and its Read report link goes directly to the publisher’s landing page. Page 3 of the updated report explicitly corrects the same child-share figure from 54% to 42%.
