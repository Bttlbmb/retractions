National Centre for Social Research · independent UK social research charity · ScotCen

Why it matters

The survey producer presents the figures as headline results for a study informing Scotland’s health strategy and national performance framework.

most frequently related to mobility (32%), stamina/breathing/fatigue (28%) and/or mental health (26%).

2024 findings · general health · Open the source ↗

The National Centre for Social Research’s Scottish Health Survey project page retains 32% for mobility impairment, 28% for stamina, breathing or fatigue and 26% for mental health impairment among adults with a long-term condition.

What changed

Corrected19 Mar 2026

The Scottish Government corrected Table 2.4 and its accompanying text in March 2026. The revised estimates for the same three categories are 41%, 37% and 32%.

Limits and context

The percentages refer to adults who report a long-term condition. Respondents could report more than one kind of impairment. The correction applies to individual categories. The separate 50% headline is the share of all adults with a long-term condition.
