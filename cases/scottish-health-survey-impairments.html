STV coverage retains the original Scottish impairment estimates

STV News · PA Media·21 October 2025

Corrected19 Mar 2026

Still says

mobility (32%), stamina, breathing or fatigue (28%) and/or mental health (26%).

STV News · article body · Open the source ↗

STV reports that among Scottish adults who said they had a long-term condition, 32% reported mobility impairment, 28% reported problems with stamina, breathing or fatigue, and 26% reported mental health impairment.

What changed

The Scottish Government corrected Table 2.4 and its accompanying text in March 2026. The revised estimates for the same three categories are 41%, 37% and 32%.

The percentages refer to adults who report a long-term condition. Respondents could report more than one kind of impairment. The correction applies to individual categories. STV’s headline figure of 50% refers to the share of all adults with a long-term condition.

Why it matters

The survey informs Scotland’s health strategy and national performance framework. The figures describe the kinds of impairment reported by adults living with a long-term condition.

How the claim connects to the study

The STV article names the Scottish Health Survey and reproduces the original category estimates. The government’s correction identifies Table 2.4, the chapter summary and section 2.2.4.
