An SRF archive keeps the original estimate of support for higher military spending

SRF · Echo der Zeit·9 April 2026

CorrectedApril 2026

Still says

knapp ein Viertel der Befragten möchten hingegen eher mehr Geld für die Armee ausgeben.

Echo der Zeit · chapter starting at 27:07 · Open the source ↗

In the archived introduction to a report on Swiss public spending, SRF says just under a quarter of respondents want to spend more on the military. Its original broadcast is still available.

What changed

ETH Zurich corrected the report summary from fewer than a quarter to about 40%. The underlying statistical annex already gave the correct figures. SRF acknowledged the error in its separate corrections log.

The corrected report summary describes support among respondents informed about current military spending. The survey also asked separate questions about development aid.

Why it matters

The broadcast uses the figure to compare public preferences with Parliament’s spending priorities on the military and development aid.

How the claim connects to the study

The broadcast introduces the ETH survey. SRF’s correction names the original statistic and ETH’s revised figure. ETH’s own notice describes the same report-summary error.
