An Austrian poverty facts page keeps the original estimate of 1.699 million people

Armutskonferenz · Austrian poverty facts·Current facts page · EU-SILC 2025

Corrected12 May 2026

Still says

18,8% der öster. Bevölkerung (1.699.000 Menschen) sind armuts- oder ausgrenzungsgefährdet

Daten aus EU-SILC 2025 · Open the source ↗

The Austrian Anti-Poverty Network gives 18.8%, or 1,699,000 people, as the share at risk of poverty or social exclusion in 2025. Its source link leads to the corrected report.

What changed

Statistics Austria corrected a data-linkage error and recalculated indicators based on household income. Its May 2026 revision put the estimate at 18.6%, or 1,686,000 people.

This is a combined measure of income poverty, severe material or social deprivation, and very low household work intensity. The correction is within the reported sampling interval. The same facts page separately gives 15.9% for income poverty, a narrower measure.

Why it matters

The network uses the indicator to explain poverty and social exclusion in Austria under the EU’s 2030 framework.

How the claim connects to the study

The facts page names the survey and links its revised table volume. The April release gives the exact original percentage and count. The May erratum identifies the cause, and the updated official factsheet gives both corrected values.
