Why it matters

The withdrawn review remains substantively summarized in publicly distributed parliamentary research about a contested agricultural chemical.

Still says

28 June 2016
Es gäbe keinen überzeugenden Hinweis auf direkte DNA-Schäden.

Printed/PDF page 14 · Williams, Kroes and Munro summary · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe current Bundestag research catalogue still offers a 2016 overview that summarizes the Williams, Kroes and Munro review: no convincing direct DNA-damage evidence and no demonstrated tumour effects, alongside other safety conclusions.

What changed

RetractedLate 2025

The journal withdrew the review after its editor lost confidence in the results and conclusions, citing concerns about authorship, sponsor contributions, selective evidence and potential conflicts of interest.

This is a dated 2016 selected-studies overview that includes contrary IARC and other findings and explicitly does not claim to be comprehensive or representative. Its authors summarize the review in indirect speech; the document is not a Bundestag endorsement or a new policy decision. The retraction explicitly takes no position on actual carcinogenicity and does not itself invalidate current regulatory approvals. Primary records differ on the precise late-2025 withdrawal date.

How the claim connects to the study

Page 14 names all three authors and the exact 2000 title, journal and pages immediately before the substantive safety summary. The current catalogue links this same 20-page PDF. The notice identifies the same original review; the original PubMed record marks it retracted.
