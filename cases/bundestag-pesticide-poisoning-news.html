Parliamentary news repeats the motion’s withdrawn pesticide poisoning estimate

German Bundestag · parliamentary news service

Why it matters

The official parliamentary news summary makes the motion’s export and food-import proposals accessible to the public and repeats the statistic used to support them.

Still says

22 September 2026
Die Folge seien jährlich rund 385 Millionen unbeabsichtigte akute Pestizidvergiftungen, darunter etwa 11.000 Todesfälle.

Final paragraph · summary of the motion’s grounds · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe Bundestag news service reports Die Linke’s motion and repeats its estimate of 385 million unintended acute pesticide poisonings worldwide each year, including about 11,000 fatalities.

What changed

Retracted9 Oct 2024

The journal retracted the 2020 study over the way it used lifetime or unspecified time periods to estimate annual poisonings. The authors disputed the retraction. Their separate 2026 analysis estimated 402–433 million non-fatal cases per year across its sensitivity analyses.

The news service attributes the estimates and demands to the MPs’ motion. It does not report a parliamentary decision. The authors published a separate updated review before the motion and summary appeared.

How the claim connects to the study

The summary directly links motion 21/8073. The motion cites PAN Germany’s 2023 explainer, whose reference 1 identifies the withdrawn Boedeker paper.

Boedeker, Watts, Clausing & Marquez · 2020 · BMC Public Health
The global distribution of acute unintentional pesticide poisoning: estimations based on a systematic review
