ZDF still headlines a hospital-treatment figure corrected the next day

ZDFheute·Article published 16 April 2026 · still available

Corrected17 Apr 2026

Still says

Jährlich sieben Millionen Klinikbehandlungen wegen Alkohols

Headline, introduction and main article · Open the source ↗

The headline and article report seven million alcohol-related hospital treatments each year, attributing the number to the DHS addiction yearbook.

What changed

DHS declared the seven-million figure false the following day and replaced it with a differently defined official statistic and a disease-burden statement.

The replacement describes about 283,500 patients treated in 2024 for exclusively alcohol-caused diagnoses. This is a narrower measure, so the two numbers are not a like-for-like 25-fold comparison. The erratum leaves the chapter’s overall conclusions intact and does not withdraw the separate 44,000-deaths estimate.

Why it matters

The national broadcaster presents the number prominently in reporting that also covers proposals for higher alcohol taxes and sales restrictions.

How the claim connects to the study

ZDF names DHS, its 2026 yearbook and the launch in Hamm. The same seven-million statement appears in the yearbook announcement. The formal erratum identifies the chapter, page 295 and the exact false sentence. The current ZDF article contains no corresponding update.
