DAK’s parent guide repeats the old youth gaming estimate after a correction

DAK-Gesundheit · parent guide·Updated 1 October 2026

Corrected8 Sept 2026

Still says

lag 2024 bei 3,4 Prozent, verglichen mit 4,3 Prozent (2023).

DAK parent guide · Computerspielsucht · Open the source ↗

DAK’s guide gives 4.3% as the share of children and teenagers meeting the survey criteria for a gaming disorder in 2023. It describes declines in consecutive years. The 4.3% also appears in a Bundestag expert discussion.

What changed

DAK and University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf corrected how the survey applied its thresholds. The 2023 estimate rose to 5.9%. The corrected report describes the 2022 and 2023 rates as statistically similar.

The correction concerns the 2023 survey of children aged 10–17. The estimates are based on survey responses aligned with ICD-11 criteria. The 2024 estimate of 3.4% comes from a separate wave.

Why it matters

The finding informs public information for parents and expert evidence to the Bundestag family committee on the risks of social media and gaming.

How the claim connects to the study

The guide describes the DAK and UKE survey and reproduces the original 2023 figure. The corrected report’s section 4.1.2 and its footnotes show both estimates and the revised test result. In the hearing, the research lead names the sixth wave and gives 4.3%.
