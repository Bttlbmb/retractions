MajorSwitzerland·Climate & environment

A current Swiss policy-study download retains a withdrawn global damage estimate

Swiss Federal Office for the Environment · commissioned report·Final report · 25 September 2024

Retracted3 Dec 2025

Still says

weltweiten jährlichen Schäden auf 38 Billionen Dollar geschätzt

Page 17 · footnote 9 · Open the source ↗

A footnote in an assessment of Switzerland’s options for EU deforestation rules reports US$38 trillion in annual global damage by 2050, with a range of US$19–59 trillion.

What changed

Nature retracted the paper after data and modelling problems required revisions too extensive for a correction.

The withdrawn estimate is global context in a contractor-authored report. The nearby CHF701 million estimate for Swiss external costs uses a separate emissions and carbon-cost calculation; it is not shown to depend on Kotz. The report predates the retraction, and the commissioning office states that its authors are responsible for its content.

Why it matters

The official environment-office catalogue still supplies this commissioned impact assessment for a live regulatory topic; a Swiss parliamentary answer also directs readers to the report.

How the claim connects to the study

Footnote 9 names Kotz, Levermann and Wenz (2024); the bibliography on page 123 gives the exact original Nature title and DOI. The current official study catalogue links the same 134-page PDF without a source-withdrawal update.
