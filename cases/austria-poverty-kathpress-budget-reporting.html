MinorAustria·Social policy

Reporting on Caritas’s budget concerns retains the original Austrian poverty estimate

Kathpress · published by katholisch.at

Why it matters

The reporting places the estimate in a debate over how spending cuts could affect households already experiencing poverty.

Still says

8 May 2026
2025 waren demnach in Österreich 1.699.000 Menschen (18,8 Prozent) armuts- oder ausgrenzungsgefährdet

Background to the social and budget debate · Open the source ↗

In EnglishA Kathpress report gives 1,699,000 people, or 18.8%, at risk of poverty or social exclusion in 2025 while covering Caritas’s concerns about Austria’s forthcoming budget.

What changed

Corrected12 May 2026

Statistics Austria corrected a data-linkage error and recalculated indicators based on household income. Its May 2026 revision put the estimate at 18.6%, or 1,686,000 people.

The article was published four days before the correction. The figure appears in the reporter’s background account of the survey. The text separately reports Caritas secretary-general Anna Parr’s arguments about social policy.
