MajorGermany·Climate & environment

A school climate resource retains estimates from a retracted paper

Baden-Württemberg Environment Ministry

Why it matters

The state environment ministry supplies the climate booklet through its official catalogue of classroom materials. The figures frame its lesson on the economic costs of climate change.

Still says

September 2025 · second updated edition
Auf 38 Billionen Dollar beziffern Forscher des Potsdam-Instituts für Klimafolgenforschung die weltweiten Einkommensverluste durch den Klimawandel bis 2050

Page 17 · Tun ist billiger als Nichtstun · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe school resource puts worldwide climate-related income losses by 2050 at US$38 trillion, equivalent to 19% of global GDP. It says limiting warming to 2°C would cost one sixth of that amount.

What changed

Retracted3 Dec 2025

Nature retracted the underlying paper after data and modelling problems required revisions too extensive for a correction.

Limits and context

The PDF edition predates the retraction and remains available. The underlying paper projected annual income losses in 2050 relative to a baseline without climate impacts. The catalogue separately identifies an older print edition.

How the claim connects to the study

Page 17 names Kotz, Levermann and Wenz, the 2024 publication year and the exact Nature paper title. The current classroom catalogue directly offers this September 2025 PDF. The complete booklet contains no notice of the paper’s retraction.
