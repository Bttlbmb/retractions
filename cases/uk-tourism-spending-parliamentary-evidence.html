MinorUnited Kingdom·Public finance

DCMS parliamentary evidence retains the original £32.5 billion tourism estimate

Department for Culture, Media and Sport

Why it matters

The department uses the estimate as background to its evidence on tourism policy, visitor growth, competitiveness and the visitor economy strategy.

Still says

May 2026 submission · published 14 July 2026
in 2024, 42.6 million overseas residents came to the UK and spent £32.5 billion.

Background · footnote 3 · Open the source ↗

DCMS tells the Commons Culture, Media and Sport Committee that overseas residents made 42.6 million UK visits and spent £32.5 billion in 2024.

What changed

Corrected4 Sept 2026

The Office for National Statistics (ONS) corrected processing errors and updated the estimation methods for the same survey year. The September 2026 release lowered estimated UK spending by overseas visitors to £31.6 billion.

Limits and context

The submission predates the September 2026 correction. The rounded UK visitor count remains 42.6 million. The spending revision combines corrections and method changes for the same survey year.
