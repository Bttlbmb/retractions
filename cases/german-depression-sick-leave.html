Germany·Public health

Tagesschau retains the original 50% rise in depression-related sick leave

Tagesschau·24 March 2025

Corrected31 Mar 2025

Still says

Demnach gab es im vergangenen Jahr 50 Prozent mehr Fehltage durch Depressionen als 2023.

Tagesschau · article body · Open the source ↗

Tagesschau says depression caused about 50% more sick-leave days in 2024 than in 2023, from 122 to 183 days per 100 DAK-insured employees.

What changed

DAK withdrew the comparison because the two years used different rules to assign diagnoses. Applying the same rule to both years gives 182.2 days in 2023 and 182.6 days in 2024, a rise of 0.2%.

The report analyses absences among 2.42 million DAK-insured employees. The corrected 2024 figure still rounds to 183 days per 100 employees.

Why it matters

The national news report uses the original increase in its account of workplace mental health and support for affected employees.

How the claim connects to the study

Tagesschau names the DAK report and links its original file. DAK’s 31 March notice identifies the error, and its April replacement gives the comparable values on page 18.
