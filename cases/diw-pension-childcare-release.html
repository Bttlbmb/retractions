DIW’s public press release retains pension estimates corrected in its study

DIW Berlin · public press release

Why it matters

The research institute’s public summary supplies the original figures to journalists and readers while its linked research paper contains the corrected comparisons.

Still says

19 March 2025
Der sogenannte Motherhood Pension Gap beträgt 26 statt 31 Prozent.

Opening summary paragraph · Open the source ↗

In EnglishDIW’s release still says child-rearing credits narrow the West German gender pension gap by four points, from 41% to 37%, and the motherhood gap by five points, from 31% to 26%. Its corrected study gives reductions of nine and fourteen points.

What changed

Corrected18 Jun 2025

A code error affected the calculation without child-rearing credits. The corrected study changes the gender-gap comparison to 47% versus 38%, and the motherhood-gap comparison to 41% versus 27%.

The study concerns pension entitlements accrued at age 60 for birth cohorts 1952–1959. These are comparisons with and without existing credits. The authors retained their broad conclusion after correction. The release also discusses separate East German results.

How the claim connects to the study

The press release identifies the study and its researchers and links its DIW Wochenbericht publication record. Its first paragraph retains both original comparisons. The corrected paper and formal erratum identify the code error and revised results. The release itself has no correction notice.
