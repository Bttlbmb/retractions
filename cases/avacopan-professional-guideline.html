A professional guideline retains an avacopan recommendation after the trial’s retraction

German rheumatology society · DGRh professional guideline

Why it matters

The society supplies this professional guideline through its current clinical-guideline catalogue for treatment decisions in ANCA-associated vasculitis.

Still says

2025 professional guideline edition · recommendation dated 2023
Der Einsatz von Avacopan kann zusätzlich zu einer Remissionsinduktion mit CYC oder RTX erwogen werden, um die kumulative GC-Dosis zu reduzieren.

Page 20 / S20 · recommendation 5.4 and its evidence discussion · Open the source ↗

In EnglishRecommendation 5.4 says clinicians may consider avacopan with cyclophosphamide or rituximab to reduce cumulative glucocorticoid exposure. The evidence discussion explicitly bases it on ADVOCATE.

What changed

Retracted29 Jun 2026

NEJM retracted ADVOCATE after undisclosed endpoint reassessment following unblinding. EU authorisation was revoked on 4 August 2026 after regulators found efficacy was no longer demonstrated.

The recommendation is optional and predates the retraction. The professional text discusses limitations of ADVOCATE, including its treatment comparisons and follow-up. The society’s separate July 2026 warning advises against new prescribing. The original guideline remains available through its catalogue.

How the claim connects to the study

Recommendation 5.4 and its explanation on page 20 explicitly name ADVOCATE and cite reference 153. That reference identifies Jayne et al.’s 2021 NEJM trial by authors, full title, volume and pages. The catalogue directly supplies this 49-page professional guideline, which contains no 2026 retraction update.
