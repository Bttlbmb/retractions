MinorUnited Kingdom·Social policy

The Child Poverty Strategy retains the original count of families in B&Bs beyond six weeks

Cabinet Office · UK central government department · Child Poverty Strategy

Why it matters

The strategy uses the count in its explanation of how temporary accommodation affects children and its commitments on safe housing and preventing homelessness.

Still says

5 December 2025 · strategy
2,070 families with children have been living in B&Bs for more than six weeks

Preventing homelessness and emergency accommodation · footnote 130 · Open the source ↗

The Child Poverty Strategy gives 2,070 families with children in England staying in bed-and-breakfast (B&B) accommodation beyond six weeks, alongside the June 2025 total of 3,340 in B&Bs.

What changed

Corrected26 Feb 2026

The housing ministry corrected a recording problem in February 2026. The earlier count did not properly add together multiple short B&B placements when applying the six-week limit. The revised count for 30 June 2025 is 2,130.

Limits and context

The corrected series is an England-wide snapshot for households with children or a pregnant member. The 3,340 households in B&B accommodation of any duration in June 2025 are counted separately.
