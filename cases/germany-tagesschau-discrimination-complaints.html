A live tagesschau topic listing retains a fivefold overstatement of formal discrimination complaints

tagesschau · ARD-aktuell

Why it matters

44% is approximately 5.4 times 8.1%, materially overstating use of formal complaint mechanisms. The study informs debate on discrimination protection, support services and reform of the General Equal Treatment Act; its researchers recommend more transparent, accessible complaint routes.

In EnglishThe broadcaster's discrimination topic page still displays a video headline saying 44% of affected respondents formally complained.

What changed

Corrected2026 · exact correction insertion date unclear

tagesschau formally acknowledged that its report on the study misstated 44% as the proportion making an official complaint; the correct figure is 8.1%. It says the report and related social posts were taken offline. The underlying report independently gives 8.1% in section 3.4, printed page 68.

This is a formal correction of the broadcaster's interpretation, not a correction or retraction of the DeZIM/SOEP study. The verified surviving occurrence is a headline in an active topic listing; continued playback or the current content of that video was not verified. The corrected 20:00 broadcast page explicitly carries a warning. The correction log's 10 March timestamp identifies the original broadcast, not necessarily the date the correction was inserted. Survey interviews ran May 2022–January 2023 and asked about the preceding twelve months; they do not measure current 2026 prevalence. No deliberate concealment is established. The report’s 56% no-action share implies 44% took some form of action; official complaints were a distinct response, with multiple answers permitted.

How the claim connects to the study

The topic listing names the Antidiskriminierungsstelle study and repeats the exact official-complaint claim identified in tagesschau's own correction. Its corrected broadcast page identifies the study as Wie Deutschland Diskriminierung erlebt. Official DeZIM publication and project pages identify the Bartsch/Meksem SOEP report and link the federal agency's report. A complete third-party-hosted copy of that primary report confirms the title, authors, March 2026 publication, agency imprint and 8.1% complaint result.
