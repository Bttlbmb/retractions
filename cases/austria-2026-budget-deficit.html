Austria·Public finance

Heute reports Austria’s original €21.4 billion deficit forecast after a same-day correction

Heute·2 October 2026

Corrected2 Oct 2026

Still says

Österreichs Budgetdefizit soll 2026 bei 4,1 Prozent des BIP und damit bei 21,4 Milliarden Euro liegen.

Heute · standfirst · Open the source ↗

Heute says Austria’s 2026 general-government deficit is forecast at €21.4 billion, or 4.1% of GDP.

What changed

The Finance Ministry corrected the amount to €22 billion at 13:53 Austrian time. The forecast remained 4.1% of GDP.

Both amounts refer to the same October 2026 forecast. The ministry based it on the first eight months of federal and state budgets and the first six months of general-government results.

Why it matters

Heute uses the forecast to explain Austria’s budget consolidation and the scale of its public finances.

How the claim connects to the study

Heute attributes the calculation to the Finance Ministry. Its article was published at 15:45 Austrian time, 1 hour and 52 minutes after the ministry’s correction.
