Coalition pension-reform reporting retains the study’s old gap figures

Frankfurter Rundschau · Haufe · DIW Berlin·DIW · 19 March 2025; Haufe · 21 March 2025; FR · 16 April 2025

Corrected18 Jun 2025

Still says

nur um vier Prozentpunkte – von 41 auf 37 Prozent.

“DIW-Studie belegt: Rentenlücke bleibt trotz Reform groß” · Open the source ↗

Frankfurter Rundschau still says existing child-rearing credits narrow the West German gender pension gap by four percentage points, from 41% to 37%. The corrected study gives nine points, from 47% to 38%. Haufe and DIW’s own public release also retain the old motherhood-gap comparison.

What changed

A code error affected the calculation without child-rearing credits. The corrected study changes the gender-gap comparison to 47% versus 38%, and the motherhood-gap comparison to 41% versus 27%.

The study compares historical pension entitlements accrued at age 60 for birth cohorts 1952–1959. It does not estimate the marginal effect of the coalition’s proposed 2025 Mütterrente reform. The correction increases the estimated effect of existing credits; mothers still have lower entitlements, and the authors retain the broad conclusion. FR’s separate beneficiary and euro estimates are not affected by this correction.

Why it matters

These figures inform reporting about pension policy: a research institute’s own press release, a professional social-insurance publication and a national newspaper’s coverage of coalition reform all retain affected results.

How the claim connects to the study

All three articles identify the March 2025 DIW study and its exact old gap figures. FR links directly to DIW’s release; that release links the study. The corrected official PDF, printed page 187, states the new comparisons, and the formal erratum documents the code error and 18 June 2025 correction. No correction notice was visible in the current articles.
