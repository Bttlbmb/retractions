Coalition pension-reform reporting retains the study’s old gap figures

Frankfurter Rundschau·16 April 2025 · 09:18

Corrected18 Jun 2025

Still says

nur um vier Prozentpunkte – von 41 auf 37 Prozent.

“DIW-Studie belegt: Rentenlücke bleibt trotz Reform groß” · Open the source ↗

The newspaper says existing child-rearing credits narrow the West German gender pension gap by four percentage points, from 41% to 37%. The corrected study gives nine points, from 47% to 38%.

What changed

A code error affected the calculation without child-rearing credits. The corrected study changes the gender-gap comparison to 47% versus 38%, and the motherhood-gap comparison to 41% versus 27%.

The study compares historical pension entitlements accrued at age 60 for birth cohorts 1952–1959. It does not estimate the marginal effect of the coalition’s proposed 2025 Mütterrente reform. The correction increases the estimated effect of existing credits; mothers still have lower entitlements, and the authors retain the broad conclusion. FR’s separate beneficiary and euro estimates are not affected by this correction.

Why it matters

The article uses the study in coverage of the coalition’s proposed Mütterrente reform and the pension entitlements of mothers.

How the claim connects to the study

The article links directly to DIW’s March 2025 press release, naming the institute and reproducing its old 41%-to-37% comparison. The corrected official paper and formal erratum identify the calculation error and revised estimates. The current newspaper article carries no notice of the study correction.
