A migration policy paper’s original summary still describes a proposal as in force

Friedrich Ebert Foundation · policy research catalogue·March 2026 · original edition

CorrectedJune 2026

Still says

Seit dem 1. April 2025 gilt für neu eingereiste Geflüchtete aus der Ukraine das Asylbewerberleistungsgesetz.

March edition · catalogue abstract · Open the source ↗

The foundation’s catalogue says a different benefits law had already applied to newly arrived refugees from Ukraine since 1 April 2025. The catalogue still offers the March paper with the same wording.

What changed

The June 2026 corrected edition describes the November 2025 cabinet proposal and says the change would apply in the future. The summary and main text both change how they describe the policy’s status.

The correction changes the description of the proposal at the time of publication. The paper’s analysis of potential labour-market effects is a separate part of the report.

Why it matters

The report examines how a change in benefits could affect refugees’ access to work and social participation, particularly for women with caring responsibilities. The original and corrected editions can both be downloaded from the foundation’s Ukraine library.

How the claim connects to the study

The two editions have the same title and authors. Page 1 changes the description from a law already in force to a cabinet proposal. Page 9 of the June paper identifies it as the corrected second edition.
