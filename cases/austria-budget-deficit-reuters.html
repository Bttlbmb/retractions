Opening paragraph · Open the source ↗

A Reuters report on Onvista gives Austria’s 2026 general-government deficit forecast as 4.1% of GDP or €21.4 billion.

What changed

The Finance Ministry corrected the amount to €22 billion at 13:53 Austrian time. The forecast remained 4.1% of GDP.

The article predates the ministry’s 13:53 same-day correction. Both amounts concern the same 2026 forecast. The reported 4.1% of GDP did not change in that correction.
