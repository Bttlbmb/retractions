A water-and-energy policy paper still uses irrigation estimates from a retracted study

Danfoss · Danish industrial engineering company · English-language Impact policy paper

Why it matters

This is a mismatch in the stated research method behind global energy and emissions estimates, not a typographical correction. The policy paper still uses the retracted source to quantify the case for irrigation electrification and efficient pumping. It sits within a wider set of recommendations to governments. The notice gives no corrected estimates.

Still says

27 October 2025 launch · current Danfoss Impact Issue No. 7
replacing diesel-powered pumps with new, more efficient electrified pumps could cut energy consumption by 51%

Printed page 29 · Agriculture, reference 95; additional derived estimates on printed page 15 and reference 40 · Open the source ↗

Danfoss’s policy paper derives annual groundwater-pumping energy use (469 terawatt-hours, TWh) and CO₂ emissions (192 million tonnes) from Qin and colleagues. It cites the study for a projected 51% energy saving from switching diesel irrigation pumps to electric.

What changed

Retracted22 September 2026

Nature Communications retracted the underlying study because the authors used a global crop-water model (GCWM), contrary to their stated use of another model (WATNEEDS). The corresponding author said on behalf of the authors that they disagreed with the retraction.

Limits and context

The retraction establishes the model-method discrepancy. It does not establish that every estimate or the 51% saving is numerically false, quantify the resulting bias or disprove irrigation electrification benefits. The 469 TWh and 192-million-tonne figures are Danfoss’s own derivations. Reference 40 applies the study’s 89% groundwater-energy share to its 527 TWh and 216-million-tonne global estimates. The source paper itself is not a study of a specific German- or English-speaking country. This occurrence is an English-language international policy paper. No evidence that a government adopted its recommendations was established. Text was extracted from the current official PDF; no visual inspection is claimed.

How the claim connects to the study

The current official Danfoss PDF cites the exact Qin et al. title and DOI 10.1038/s41467-024-47383-5 in references 40 and 95. Reference 40 explains its derived pumping-energy and emissions estimates; reference 95 attaches to the diesel-to-electric-pump saving. Nature’s retraction notice explicitly targets that DOI and states the model discrepancy.

Jingxiu Qin, Weili Duan, Shan Zou, Yaning Chen, Wenjing Huang and Lorenzo Rosa · 2024 · Nature Communications
Global energy use and carbon emissions from irrigated agriculture
10.1038/s41467-024-47383-5 ↗
