German supplement sales still use a retracted vinegar trial

Naturecan · German online shop

Why it matters

The trial supports a German gummies sales page with one-time purchase and recurring-subscription options.

Still says

Live sales page · no publication date shown
mit Verbesserungen der anthropometrischen und metabolischen Messwerte einherging

Zusätzliche Informationen · study description · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe gummies sales page presents the withdrawn trial as finding improved weight-related and metabolic measures compared with placebo, without significant reported adverse events.

What changed

Retracted23 Sept 2025

BMJ withdrew the trial after results could not be replicated and analytical errors and unreliable data undermined the findings.

Limits and context

The trial tested liquid vinegar, not these gummies. The sales page cites other research too. This case concerns the affirmative use of this withdrawn trial, rather than a finding about the product’s overall efficacy.

How the claim connects to the study

The product’s study table gives the trial title and a direct BMJ link containing its exact article identifier. The 120 participants, Lebanese study population and 12-week design match the retracted paper. No retraction notice appears in the table or the full product page.

Abou-Khalil et al. · 2024 · BMJ Nutrition, Prevention & Health
Apple cider vinegar for weight management in Lebanese adolescents and young adults with overweight and obesity: a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled study
10.1136/bmjnph-2023-000823 ↗
