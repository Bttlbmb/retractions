A supplement seller’s 2026 MS advice retains a retracted disability result

Nordic Oil · German online shop·Advice page updated 18 August 2026

Retracted14 Aug 2025

Still says

Hinweis auf Nutzen; kleine Stichprobe.

Interventionsstudien mit Probiotika · first study-table row · Open the source ↗

The study table presents EDSS score changes of −0.3 versus +0.1 points as evidence of benefit in multiple sclerosis.

What changed

Clinical Nutrition retracted the trial after the integrity of its table data could not be confirmed and its results and conclusions were judged unreliable.

The page cites other studies and acknowledges limited evidence. The trial tested a different formulation. The authors dispute the retraction.

Why it matters

The German-market advice includes a probiotic purchase offer with an Add to cart button.

How the claim connects to the study

Five direct links point to PMID 27669638. The 60-person sample, 12-week duration and EDSS changes match the trial. The page displays an 18 August 2026 update date and no retraction note.

Kouchaki et al. · 2017 · Clinical Nutrition
Clinical and metabolic response to probiotic supplementation in patients with multiple sclerosis: A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial
