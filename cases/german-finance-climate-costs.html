Germany·Climate & environment

A 2026 finance-policy handbook teaches estimates from a retracted climate paper

Heinrich-Böll-Stiftung / FiscalFuture·Handbook published February 2026

Retracted3 Dec 2025

Still says

um 19 Prozent sinken

Page 63 · state financing options · Open the source ↗

The handbook uses a 19% global income-loss estimate and a six-to-one damage-versus-mitigation cost comparison to argue for investment today.

What changed

Nature retracted the underlying paper after data and modelling problems required revisions too extensive for a correction.

The affected figures come from Kotz et al. The original 19% estimate compared future income with a no-climate-impact baseline; it was not an absolute fall from present income. Other evidence on the page has separate sources.

Why it matters

The foundation and FiscalFuture distribute the handbook for political education on public investment, borrowing and fiscal policy. Its publication follows the retraction.

How the claim connects to the study

Two actual hyperlinks on handbook page 63 lead directly to the original Nature paper. The imprint dates the handbook to February 2026, after the 3 December 2025 retraction. The current workshop page offers this PDF and trainer materials. The handbook contains no retraction note.
