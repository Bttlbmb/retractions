A professional tax article retains HMRC’s original capital gains figures

Professional Paraplanner · Jason Hollands·29 July 2025

Corrected27 Aug 2026

Still says

the total CGT liability was £12.1 billion for 378,000 taxpayers, realised on £65.9 billion of gains.

Opening account of HMRC’s annual release · Open the source ↗

Professional Paraplanner reports £12.1 billion in UK capital gains tax liabilities, 378,000 taxpayers and £65.9 billion in gains for 2023/24.

What changed

HMRC says a processing error omitted about 20,000 taxpayers from its 2025 release. The August 2026 publication fixes the error and incorporates the usual updates to returns. For the same year it reports £12.773 billion in liabilities and £69.861 billion in gains.

The figures measure gains and liabilities for the 2023/24 tax year. Cash receipts are a separate measure. The revision includes updates to provisional figures as well as the correction.

Why it matters

The article uses the figures to question the revenue effect of lowering the tax-free allowance and discuss possible changes at the next Budget.
