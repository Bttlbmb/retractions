ORF’s budget report keeps the ministry’s original €21.4 billion forecast

ORF·2 October 2026 · 11:25 Austrian time

Corrected2 Oct 2026

Still says

einem Budgetdefizit von 4,1 Prozent bzw. von 21,4 Mrd. Euro im heurigen Jahr

Opening paragraph · Open the source ↗

ORF reports that Austria expects a 2026 general-government deficit of 4.1% of GDP or €21.4 billion and attributes the estimate to the Finance Ministry.

What changed

The Finance Ministry corrected the amount to €22 billion at 13:53 Austrian time. The forecast remained 4.1% of GDP.

The article predates the ministry’s 13:53 same-day correction. The correction changes the monetary amount of the same forecast to €22 billion while retaining 4.1% of GDP.

Why it matters

The public broadcaster uses the forecast to explain budget consolidation, the performance of different levels of government and the absence of additional spending room.

How the claim connects to the study

The article explicitly attributes the calculation to the Finance Ministry’s same-day statement. The original and corrected ministry releases identify the exact monetary error.
