A Treasury response to Parliament retains the old NHS productivity figure

HM Treasury · response to the Public Accounts Committee·15 May 2025 · command paper CP 1328

Corrected17 Mar 2026

Still says

productivity improved by 1.9% between 2021-22 and 2022-23.

Section 3.2 · printed page 4 · Open the source ↗

The Treasury says NHS productivity in England improved by 1.9% between 2021/22 and 2022/23. It attributes that estimate to the Office for National Statistics.

What changed

The ONS identified a spreadsheet formula that used the wrong source data to adjust healthcare costs for price changes. Fixing it raised estimated productivity growth for 2022/23 from 1.9% to 2.9%.

The figures estimate annual quality-adjusted healthcare productivity growth in England for 2022/23.

Why it matters

The Treasury cites the figure in its response to a parliamentary recommendation to set out how the NHS would meet future productivity commitments.

How the claim connects to the study

The Treasury attributes the 1.9% to the latest ONS annual publication. ONS Figure 3 isolates the effect of the correction for that exact period and shows 2.9%. The corrected dataset also gives 2.9%.
