MajorAustria·Climate & environment

A climate-policy article still presents a retracted review as proof that CO₂ does not drive climate change

Report24 · Austrian online portal · Heinz Steiner

Why it matters

The article uses the review to challenge the scientific premise of decarbonisation, not a narrow subsidiary estimate. The retraction, for problems with its conclusions and interpretation, removes this review as dependable support for that political argument. The headline and endorsement remain without a retraction warning in the retrieved article.

Still says

2 October 2023 · article remains publicly available
Nun zeigt eine weitere Studie, dass das Kohlendioxid in Bezug auf den Klimawandel faktisch keine Rolle spielt.

Opening paragraph below the headline · Open the source ↗

In EnglishReport24 says a review by Stuart A. Harris demonstrates that atmospheric CO₂ is not responsible for climate change, presents it as scientific opposition to economic decarbonisation, and predicts cooling and glacier growth.

What changed

Retracted16 Sept 2026

Atmosphere retracted Harris’s review on 16 September 2026. An investigation found insufficient assessment of the literature, unsupported conclusions, inaccurate data interpretation and problems with using and attributing references. The Editorial Board said the author’s responses did not resolve the concerns. The Board said it had lost confidence in the findings’ integrity and reliability.

Limits and context

This was a literature review, not a new experiment on the effect of CO₂. The notice says the author was informed but did not comment on the decision. It does not say whether he agreed. No misconduct finding or deliberate concealment is established. The case does not independently adjudicate every linked climate claim. The Daily Sceptic’s related article now discloses the retraction and is excluded.

How the claim connects to the study

Report24 names Stuart A. Harris and Atmosphere and links its phrase ‘eine weitere Studie’ directly to DOI 10.3390/atmos14081244. Clicking that link resolves to the publisher’s exact article, now titled RETRACTED and bearing the 16 September 2026 notice link. The publisher’s issue 17(9) identifies the separate retraction as article 900, DOI 10.3390/atmos17090900. Report24’s imprint locates its editorial office in Linz, Austria.
