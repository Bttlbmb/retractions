A public autism-drug explainer retains a positive trial result after retraction

The Independent · syndicated science explainer

Why it matters

The explainer addresses families’ questions about a drug promoted in a major political announcement.

Still says

23 September 2025
Children given folinic acid showed more pronounced improvement when compared with those who took the placebo.

What does the evidence say? · paragraph on the 2024 study · Open the source ↗

The evidence section describes greater improvement with folinic acid than placebo in a 2024 trial of 80 autistic children. It does not flag the trial’s subsequent retraction.

What changed

Retracted29 Jan 2026

The editor retracted the trial after errors in reported results and statistical concerns. An independent review could not reproduce the findings from the supplied dataset.

The article explicitly says the evidence is insufficient to change medical practice. This case concerns its retained trial paragraph. The newspaper separately reported the retraction in March 2026. The finding does not invalidate other trials or treatments for established cerebral folate deficiency.

How the claim connects to the study

The Independent credits and links the original Conversation article. Its “2024 study” anchor links directly to the retracted DOI. The original primary abstract confirms the distinctive age range, cohort, dose and outcome.
