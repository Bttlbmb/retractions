MinorUnited Kingdom·Public finance

A TUC report on interest rates still cites the original factory-gate inflation peak

Trades Union Congress · UK federation of trade unions

Why it matters

The report cites the peak to show how far cost pressures had fallen as it questions the Bank of England’s rapid interest rate increases.

Still says

3 August 2023
producer price output inflation is only 0.1 per cent, down from the recent peak of 19.6 per cent in July 2022.

Jobs and Economy Monitor · page 9 · Open the source ↗

The Trades Union Congress’s Jobs and Economy Monitor gives 19.6% as the peak annual growth in UK factory-gate prices in July 2022.

What changed

Corrected22 Oct 2025

The Office for National Statistics (ONS) corrected an error in how it linked successive years of price data and updated the historical index weights. The final corrected July 2022 rate was 18.0%.

Limits and context

The affected statistic is the annual rate for UK producer output prices in July 2022. The chain-linking fix alone produced an interim estimate of 20.1%. The subsequent weight changes brought it to 18.0%.

How the claim connects to the study

The date, measure and original rate on page 9 match the ONS output-price series in Table 4 of the June 2023 bulletin. The ONS correction explicitly compares that value with the corrected result.
