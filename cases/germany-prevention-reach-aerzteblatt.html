Medical reporting retains the original prevention programme reach and growth rate

Deutsches Ärzteblatt

Why it matters

The report summary informs a medical readership about the scale of statutory health insurance prevention activities and spending.

Still says

29 November 2023
Laut Präventionsbericht wurden damit im vergangenen Jahr rund 9,3 Millionen Menschen in mehr als 50.000 Lebenswelten erreicht.

Community health promotion activities · Open the source ↗

In EnglishDeutsches Ärzteblatt attributes 9.3 million people reached and a 55% annual increase to the 2023 Prevention Report’s account of community programmes.

What changed

CorrectedSeptember 2024

The report’s September 2024 erratum fixes a calculation error. Estimated direct reach fell from 9,262,221 to 8,373,303, and the year-on-year increase from 55% to 40%.

The figures estimate how many people were reached by prevention activities in community settings. The count of settings is a separate measure.

How the claim connects to the study

The article identifies the 2023 Prevention Report and reports its exact original reach and annual increase. The corrected edition’s erratum gives both versions.
