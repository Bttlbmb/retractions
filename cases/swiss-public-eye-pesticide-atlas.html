The Swiss Pesticide Atlas still presents the withdrawn global poisoning estimate

Public Eye · Heinrich Böll Foundation·February 2022 · Swiss edition

Retracted9 Oct 2024

Still says

385 Millionen Menschen erkranken jährlich an Pestizidvergiftungen.

Printed page 18 · health effects · Open the source ↗

The Swiss Pesticide Atlas states that 385 million people become ill from pesticide poisoning each year. Public Eye continues to offer the edition for download and free print ordering.

What changed

The journal retracted the 2020 study over the way it used lifetime or unspecified time periods to estimate annual poisonings. The authors disputed the retraction. Their separate 2026 analysis estimated 402–433 million non-fatal cases per year across its sensitivity analyses.

The atlas predates the October 2024 retraction and presents the 2020 review. The authors’ September 2026 publication is a separate updated analysis with new data and sensitivity analyses.

Why it matters

The public education atlas presents the figure in its account of the worldwide health consequences of pesticide use.

How the claim connects to the study

Pages 18–19 give the estimate and name Boedeker and colleagues as the source of the global map. The source list on page 48 identifies the same 2020 study.

Boedeker, Watts, Clausing & Marquez · 2020 · BMC Public Health
The global distribution of acute unintentional pesticide poisoning: estimations based on a systematic review
10.1186/s12889-020-09939-0 ↗
