A parliamentary research brief retains a retracted ChatGPT learning result

German Bundestag · Scientific Services

Why it matters

The study summary remains in an official parliamentary research resource on AI in education, available to inform lawmakers and public debate.

Still says

Brief completed 25 November 2025 · still distributed
einen großen positiven Einfluss auf die Lernleistung

Page 11 · Wang and Fan study summary · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe brief summarizes a large positive learning effect and says the findings could support ChatGPT’s appropriate integration in schools and universities.

What changed

Retracted22 Apr 2026

The journal retracted the meta-analysis because discrepancies undermined confidence in its analysis and conclusions.

The brief attributes the findings to the researchers. It is a Scientific Services briefing, not a parliamentary resolution. Its completion date precedes the retraction; the issue is the version still supplied today.

How the claim connects to the study

Page 11 names Wang and Fan, gives the full paper title and links the exact DOI. The complete current PDF contains no retraction update. Although the cover carries © 2026, page 2 gives the actual completion date as 25 November 2025.
