MajorGermany·Media & democracy

A public-broadcasting story retains council figures corrected by the study’s publisher

Frankfurter Neue Presse / IPPEN·Article updated 3 April 2025 · still available

Corrected11 Jun 2025

Still says

Am geringsten ist er bei Radio Bremen (15 Prozent)

Comparison of political connections across broadcasting councils · Open the source ↗

The article puts politically connected council members at 15% for Radio Bremen and 56% for Deutschlandradio, using an OBS study’s classification.

What changed

The publisher’s correction changes those figures to 27% and 53%, respectively. The aggregate for all councils also changes from 41% to 42%.

These are corrected counts under the study’s definition of political connections. They do not establish a constitutional violation or measure actual political influence. The article includes methodological criticism from ARD; it does not report the later numerical corrections.

Why it matters

The numbers support prominent German political reporting about public broadcasters’ independence and the composition of their oversight bodies.

How the claim connects to the study

The article links the Otto-Brenner-Stiftung study. The publisher’s correction list explicitly replaces the same table’s Radio Bremen and Deutschlandradio percentages; these are corrections to the original counts, rather than later membership changes.
