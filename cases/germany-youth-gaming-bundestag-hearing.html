A Bundestag hearing record retains the original youth gaming-disorder estimate

Rainer Thomasius · evidence to the Bundestag family committee

Why it matters

The witness presents the estimate as part of evidence on digital media risks and recommendations for prevention, parental controls and child protection.

Still says

15 May 2024 · committee hearing
eine Prävalenz von zuletzt ganz rechts angegeben 4,3 Prozent der zehn- bis siebzehnjährigen Kinder und Jugendlichen in Deutschland

Printed pages 8–9 · Rainer Thomasius’s evidence · Open the source ↗

In EnglishResearch lead Rainer Thomasius tells the Bundestag family committee that the September 2023 survey found 4.3% of children aged 10–17 meeting the criteria for pathological gaming. The original results also appear in the hearing materials.

What changed

Corrected8 Sept 2026

DAK and University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf corrected how the survey applied its thresholds. The 2023 estimate rose to 5.9%. The corrected report describes the 2022 and 2023 rates as statistically similar.

The record preserves the witness’s 2024 presentation of the sixth survey wave. The correction concerns the 2023 survey. The hearing also discusses separate social-media and streaming estimates.

How the claim connects to the study

Thomasius identifies the DAK-commissioned longitudinal study, sixth wave, September 2023 fieldwork and 1,083 respondents. The corrected report identifies the original 4.3% and revised 5.9%.
