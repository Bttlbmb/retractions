Haringey’s 2026 evidence review repeats the old national B&B count after correction

Haringey Council · homelessness evidence review

Why it matters

The review supplies evidence for the council’s 2026–27 homelessness strategy. Cabinet formally noted the review and separately approved the strategy.

Still says

10 March 2026 · cabinet papers
2,070 families are currently living in B&B-type accommodation and hotels beyond the 6-week statutory limit.

Page 9 · National Plan to End Homelessness · Open the source ↗

Haringey’s homelessness evidence review repeats 2,070 families staying in B&B-type accommodation beyond six weeks in its summary of the National Plan to End Homelessness.

What changed

Corrected26 Feb 2026

The housing ministry corrected a recording problem in February 2026. The earlier count did not properly add together multiple short B&B placements when applying the six-week limit. The revised count for 30 June 2025 is 2,130.

The figure is national background in the local evidence review. The document attributes the section to the December 2025 national plan. The record does not establish that this count drove a particular local decision.

How the claim connects to the study

The review’s National Plan section repeats the original national figure and wording. That plan identifies the original MHCLG series, whose table TA1 and formal notice give the corrected 2,130.
