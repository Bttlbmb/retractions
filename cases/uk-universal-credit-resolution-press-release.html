MajorUnited Kingdom·Social policy

A Universal Credit press release retains a majority claim beside the corrected percentage

Resolution Foundation · press release

Why it matters

The release presents proposals to reform Universal Credit and uses the scale of the child population affected to argue that the Government should act.

Still says

29 January 2026
Universal Credit now supports more than half of all children in the UK

Quotation attributed to Lindsay Judge, Research Director · Open the source ↗

The Resolution Foundation press release says half of children live in families receiving Universal Credit. A quotation from its research director says more than half, while a separate paragraph now gives the corrected 42%.

What changed

CorrectedCorrection date unclear

The corrected report lowers that share from 54% to 42%. Its correction notice explicitly identifies the original figure as wrong; the other figures and analysis are unchanged.

The press release is partly updated. Its body correctly gives 42%, while the opening and the research director’s quotation retain half or more than half. The 6.5 million child count was not changed. The correction notice prints 4 February 2025 despite the report being dated January 2026, so its exact date remains unresolved.

How the claim connects to the study

The press release identifies Listen and learn, the authors’ policy proposals and the child-share statistic. Page 3 of the updated report explicitly corrects the original 54% share to 42%.
