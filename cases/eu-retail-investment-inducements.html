An EU advisory opinion retains the original investment-cost comparison

European Commission · Financial Services User Group

Why it matters

The advisory group uses the commissioned study’s estimate to support an EU-wide ban on payments that can influence investment advice.

Still says

17 November 2022
the products with inducements are on average 35% more expensive for the consumer than products on which no inducements are paid.

Advisory opinion · printed page 4 · Open the source ↗

The EU’s Financial Services User Group cites an average cost difference of 35% for retail financial products sold with commissions or similar payments.

What changed

Corrected8 Feb 2023

The European Commission says its contractor Kantar acknowledged an error. For the study’s sample of 176 products, the corrected cost difference was 24–26%. The Commission used that range in its later impact assessment.

Limits and context

The comparison concerns the costs of 176 products in the study sample with and without inducements.
