United Kingdom·Public health

Scotland’s drugs mission report repeats a corrected estimate

Scottish Government · National Mission on Drugs·15 October 2026

Corrected23 Jun 2026

Still says

the estimated number of people with opioid dependence in Scotland was 43,400

Section 2.2 · Prevalence of opioid dependence · Open the source ↗

The National Mission on Drugs progress report gives an estimated 43,400 people with opioid dependence in Scotland in 2023/24 and links the Public Health Scotland study.

What changed

Public Health Scotland corrected the count to 43,600 in June 2026, almost four months before the mission report appeared. It describes the error as one of presentation in the summary and publication page.

The count refers to people aged 15–69 and is rounded to the nearest hundred. Public Health Scotland reports 1.13% and a 95% credible interval of 42,000–45,400.

Reported figures

Both estimates are reported with a prevalence of 1.13%.

Why it matters

The estimate is a population measure used to provide context for Scotland’s national drugs strategy and its annual assessment of progress.

How the claim connects to the study

The progress report links directly to the study and gives its original count for the same survey period. The formal correction on that publication page states the old and new counts.
