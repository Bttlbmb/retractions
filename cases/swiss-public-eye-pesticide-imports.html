A Swiss import-policy explainer retains a withdrawn pesticide poisoning estimate

Public Eye

Why it matters

The article uses the estimated global burden when explaining proposals to restrict residues in imported food and the risks of producing those imports abroad.

Still says

19 May 2023 · updated 26 May 2023
Weltweit vergiften sich laut einer vom UN Umweltprogramm UNEP zitierten Metastudie von 2020 jährlich bis 385 Millionen Menschen an Pestiziden

Section on risks in producing countries · Open the source ↗

In EnglishPublic Eye cites up to 385 million people poisoned by pesticides annually in an explainer arguing for Swiss restrictions on residues from pesticides banned domestically.

What changed

Retracted9 Oct 2024

The journal retracted the 2020 study over the way it used lifetime or unspecified time periods to estimate annual poisonings. The authors disputed the retraction. Their separate 2026 analysis estimated 402–433 million non-fatal cases per year across its sensitivity analyses.

The article predates the October 2024 retraction. The estimate concerns poisonings from many sources worldwide. It does not estimate the share caused by Swiss imports. The authors published a separate updated analysis in September 2026.

How the claim connects to the study

The linked words “Metastudie von 2020” lead directly to the retracted Boedeker paper. The original annual estimate and study year match that paper.

Boedeker, Watts, Clausing & Marquez · 2020 · BMC Public Health
The global distribution of acute unintentional pesticide poisoning: estimations based on a systematic review
