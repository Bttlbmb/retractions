MajorGermany·Climate & environment

A drought article still reports figures from a study retracted in September 2026

DIE ZEIT · German weekly newspaper

Why it matters

A major German news outlet uses the figures to explain drought risks and adaptation, with a German research co-author and the country’s dry spring as context.

Still says

Article published 6 June 2025 · still available
Seit 1981 hat so die Schwere von Dürren weltweit um durchschnittlich 40 Prozent zugenommen.

Explanation of the Nature study’s findings · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe article reports that global drought severity has increased by 40% since 1981 because of the atmosphere’s increased demand for moisture.

What changed

Retracted2 Sept 2026

The authors retracted the paper after missing area weighting and other processing errors affected its numerical results; a revised analysis required peer review.

Limits and context

The article predates the retraction and remains without a status update. The notice does not provide a replacement percentage or establish that warming has no effect on drought.

How the claim connects to the study

The article names Nature, lead author Solomon Gebrechorkos and German co-author Jian Peng, translates the study title and reports its headline findings. It has no direct study hyperlink. The retraction identifies errors affecting study-wide averages and regional time periods.
