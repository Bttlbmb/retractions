An energy-practitioner presentation retains a withdrawn drought-severity result

Frank Wagener · Hochschule Trier / IfaS · FNR webinar·19 November 2025 · webinar presentation

Retracted2 Sept 2026

Still says

Seit 1981 Schwere von Dürren weltweit um 40 Prozent zugenommen.

Slide 5 · Wetterextreme: Dürre · Open the source ↗

The presentation says global drought severity increased by 40% since 1981 and that the drought-affected area in 2022 was three quarters larger. It identifies the Nature paper as its source.

What changed

The authors retracted the paper after missing area weighting and other processing errors affected its numerical results; a revised analysis required peer review.

The talk predates the September 2026 retraction. The affected figures provide climate-risk context for the presentation. Its other farming and energy material has separate sources.

Why it matters

FNR supplies the presentation through a webinar for municipalities, operators, planners and potential investors considering renewable-energy and land-use projects.

How the claim connects to the study

Slide 5 names Gebrechorkos et al., gives the original Nature title and links the exact DOI. The official FNR webinar archive identifies Frank Wagener of Hochschule Trier’s IfaS and directly supplies this presentation. The complete file contains no retraction notice.
