MajorGermany·Climate & environment

A German tree-planting appeal retains a climate ranking formally corrected in 2020

VERVEE / Planted · Cologne

Why it matters

The statement is used on an active German tree-planting fundraising page with donation and contribution options, rather than only in an old news report.

Still says

Undated fundraising page · currently active
Aufforstung ist die effektivste Maßnahme, um dem Klimawandel entgegenzuwirken.

Opening study-based explanation · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe page says a 2019 ETH Zürich study shows that afforestation is the most effective measure against climate change.

What changed

Corrected29 May 2020

The formal erratum says that ranking was incorrect. The intended claim concerned carbon drawdown potential, not priority over cutting emissions or protecting existing ecosystems.

This is an older correction with a current German fundraising use. The erratum does not withdraw the whole restoration study. The organisation is a charitable gGmbH, and its FAQ says individual tree contributions are not counted against a personal carbon balance.

How the claim connects to the study

The page directly links the 2019 Science paper. The formal May 2020 erratum specifically corrects its original claim to be the most effective climate solution. The current German appeal repeats that broad ranking without identifying the correction.
