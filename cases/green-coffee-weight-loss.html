International·Health

A retracted weight-loss trial still supports green coffee sales

Research Verified·Undated sales page

Retracted16 Oct 2014

Still says

participants lost an average of 17 pounds each and 16% body fat after taking the supplement

What’s In The Clinical Study · Open the source ↗

The sales page says participants lost an average of 17 pounds and 16% body fat, and links the trial as support for its formulation.

What changed

The authors retracted the trial because they could not assure the validity of the sponsor’s data.

This finding concerns the withdrawn trial used to substantiate the sales claim. The page’s general statement that results may vary does not identify the retraction.

Why it matters

The result is used in a purchase guide beside prices, package recommendations and Add to cart buttons.

How the claim connects to the study

The sales page links an unmarked PDF. The downloaded file identifies Vinson, Burnham and Nagendran’s 2012 trial and DOI 10.2147/DMSO.S27665 on its first page.

Vinson, Burnham & Nagendran · 2012 · Diabetes, Metabolic Syndrome and Obesity
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, linear dose, crossover study to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a green coffee bean extract in overweight subjects
