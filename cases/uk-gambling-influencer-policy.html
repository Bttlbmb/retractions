ModerateUnited Kingdom·Public health

A gambling policy report still presents findings from a retracted 41-study review

GambleAware · Social Finance

Why it matters

The report informs proposals for gambling advertising rules, digital literacy education and youth protection. Its literature review is still presented as part of that evidence.

Still says

September 2025 · current research-library download
The literature review included a total of 41 studies, spanning from 2015 to May 2024.

Page 15 · chapter 3: Learnings from the existing research · Open the source ↗

The report presents a 41-study review as evidence about young people’s exposure to influencer gambling content and its effects on attitudes and engagement.

What changed

Retracted9 Feb 2026

The journal retracted the review in February 2026 over methodological and data concerns. The authors confirmed that ten included studies did not meet their eligibility criteria; editors lost confidence in the review’s rigour.

The authors disagree with the retraction. This case concerns the withdrawn review summarised in chapter 3. The report’s separate survey, content analysis and qualitative research were not retracted by this notice, and its overall policy recommendations were not thereby disproved.

How the claim connects to the study

Chapter 3 explicitly summarises a separately published academic paper. Footnote 32 identifies Bolat et al. and links the exact DOI named in the retraction. Pages 15–17 retain the review’s study count and synthesis; the current library page supplies this PDF.
