A German GP summary retains safety figures from a trial replaced in October 2026

mgb medizin · Der Allgemeinarzt

Why it matters

The article supplies German GPs with treatment and safety information about medicines being studied for Long COVID fatigue, linking the superseded trial.

Still says

Article published 30 April 2026 · still available
bei Fluvoxamin (20,0%) seltener auf als bei Metformin (28,8%) oder Placebo (29,7%)

Final results sentence · adverse events · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe summary still reports adverse events in 20.0% of the fluvoxamine group, 28.8% of the metformin group and 29.7% of the placebo group.

What changed

Replaced6 Oct 2026

The replacement reports 24.0%, two metformin dose groups at 45.1% and 42.5%, and 35.5% for placebo. The metformin findings are now described as inconclusive.

The trial was retracted and simultaneously republished in revised form. Its fluvoxamine efficacy result remains. The replacement separates two metformin doses, so the rates are shown with those groups intact. Reported adverse events are not automatically events caused by treatment.

The revised report distinguishes the higher- and lower-dose metformin groups. These are the publications’ reported figures, without calculating a single before-and-after increase.

How the claim connects to the study

The German summary directly links DOI 10.7326/ANNALS-25-03959 and gives the same 399-participant trial and original rates. PubMed links that record to the 6 October notice. The revised trial has a new DOI, 10.7326/ANNALS-26-02144, and its abstract gives the replacement figures and inconclusive metformin assessment.
