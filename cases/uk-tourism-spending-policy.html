MinorUnited Kingdom·Public finance

A government-commissioned review keeps the old £32.5 billion tourism estimate

Department for Culture, Media and Sport · Ipsos review

Why it matters

The commissioned review uses the figure to describe tourism’s economic contribution in a framework for assessing UK soft power.

Still says

20 October 2026 · updated 3 November 2026
42.6 million international visits in 2024, generating £32.5 billion in spending.

Section 11.1 · footnote 112 · Open the source ↗

The soft power evidence review uses £32.5 billion as the amount spent in the UK by international visitors in 2024. The figure appears twice in its account of tourism’s economic contribution.

What changed

Corrected4 Sept 2026

The ONS corrected processing errors and updated the estimation methods for the same survey year. The September 2026 release lowered estimated UK spending by overseas visitors to £31.6 billion.

The review was commissioned to inform policy and published after the correction. Its research period ended in May 2026. The UK headline still rounds to 42.6 million visits. The revision combines several corrections and method changes, so the contribution of each to the £0.9 billion change cannot be separated from the published totals.

How the claim connects to the study

The review cites the ONS annual 2024 overseas travel and tourism estimates at footnotes 112 and 121. The superseded ONS article lists £32.5 billion in its main points. The corrected version gives £31.6 billion for the same UK-wide measure and reporting year.
