A Bundestag question retains the original investment-cost comparison in the published reply

CDU/CSU · Bundestag question 20/5487 and reply 20/5905·2 February 2023 question · 6 March 2023 published reply

Corrected8 Feb 2023

Still says

eine Studie, der zufolge Kleinanleger im Rahmen von Provisionsberatungen im Schnitt 35 Prozent teurere Produkte kauften.

Page 1 · questioners’ preamble · Open the source ↗

The CDU/CSU questioners’ preamble reports an EU commissioner citing a study in which investors bought products costing 35% more under commission-based advice. The preamble remains reproduced in the government’s published reply.

What changed

The European Commission says its contractor Kantar acknowledged an error. For the study’s sample of 176 products, the corrected cost difference was 24–26%. The Commission used that range in its later impact assessment.

The original question predates the Commission’s 8 February 2023 correction. The March reply reproduces the questioners’ account of the commissioner’s earlier statement. The cost comparison concerns the study’s 176-product sample.

Why it matters

The question shows how the original comparison entered German parliamentary scrutiny of a possible EU ban on commissions for investment advice.

How the claim connects to the study

Questions 29 and 30 and the government’s replies identify the same Kantar-led commissioned study. The Commission’s notice explicitly corrects its 35% estimate to 24–26%.
