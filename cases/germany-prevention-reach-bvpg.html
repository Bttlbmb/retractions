Germany·Public health

A prevention association’s report summary keeps the old 9.3 million reach estimate

Federal Association for Prevention and Health Promotion · BVPG·11 December 2023 · updated 19 December 2023

CorrectedSeptember 2024

Still says

Damit wurden 9,3 Millionen Menschen in mehr als 50.000 Lebenswelten erreicht.

Primary prevention and health promotion · Open the source ↗

BVPG’s summary of the 2023 Prevention Report says prevention activities reached 9.3 million people in community settings in 2022, 55% more than in 2021.

What changed

The report’s September 2024 erratum fixes a calculation error. Estimated direct reach fell from 9,262,221 to 8,373,303, and the year-on-year increase from 55% to 40%.

The figures estimate how many people were reached by prevention activities in community settings. The count of settings is a separate measure.

Why it matters

The national prevention association uses the figures to describe the reach of statutory health insurance investment in health promotion.

How the claim connects to the study

The summary names the report and links the GKV and MD Bund publication page. The corrected report’s erratum gives the original and revised reach and growth rate.
