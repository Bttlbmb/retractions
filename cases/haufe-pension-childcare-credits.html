Der Gender Pension Gap hat sich kaum verringert · Open the source ↗

In EnglishHaufe reports that child-rearing credits reduce the West German gender pension gap from 41% to 37% and the gap between mothers and childless women from 31% to 26%. The corrected study gives 47% to 38% and 41% to 27%.

What changed

Corrected18 Jun 2025

A code error affected the calculation without child-rearing credits. The corrected study changes the gender-gap comparison to 47% versus 38%, and the motherhood-gap comparison to 41% versus 27%.

The study compares pension entitlements accrued at age 60 for birth cohorts 1952–1959. The figures compare existing credits with a hypothetical calculation excluding them. The correction increases their estimated effect while preserving the study’s broad conclusion.

How the claim connects to the study

The article names DIW Berlin and its research partners, identifies the birth cohorts and reproduces both original West German comparisons. Its source line identifies the study institute. The corrected study and formal erratum give the new estimates and explain the calculation error. The current article carries no correction notice.
