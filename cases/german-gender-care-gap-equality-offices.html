Municipal equality offices’ facts page still calls the original care-gap figure current

Federal Association of Municipal Equality Offices · BAG

Why it matters

The association uses the estimate to explain economic disadvantages from unpaid care, including effects on paid work, financial independence and pensions.

Still says

Current equality facts page · cites 2024 sources
Der Gender Care Gap beträgt aktuell 43,8 Prozent.

Unpaid care work and its economic consequences · Open the source ↗

In EnglishBAG’s public facts page describes the gender care gap as currently 43.8% and gives a daily unpaid-work difference of 77 minutes.

What changed

Corrected28 Mar 2024

Destatis corrected a data-processing error in March 2024. The estimate rose to 44.3%, or 79 minutes a day. A separate June 2025 revision using census-based weights put it at 43.4%, or 76 minutes.

Limits and context

All three estimates are for 2022 and compare average time spent on unpaid work by women and men aged 18 or older. The March 2024 correction and the June 2025 census reweighting are separate events.
