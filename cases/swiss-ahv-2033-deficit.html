ModerateSwitzerland·Public finance

A Swiss pension explainer retains the old CHF 7.3 billion deficit forecast

Blick · pension financing explainer

Why it matters

The explainer uses the forecasts to show the scale of the financing decision and compare proposals to fund the extra pension payment.

Still says

28 March 2024
Das Minus würde bis ins Jahr 2033 auf 7,3 Milliarden Franken anwachsen.

“Was würde ohne Massnahmen passieren?” · Open the source ↗

In EnglishBlick’s explainer says the Swiss old-age pension system would run an annual CHF 7.3 billion shortfall in 2033 if the 13th pension payment went ahead without additional funding. It also gives the old forecast of 45% for fund assets relative to annual spending.

What changed

Corrected16 Sept 2024

Switzerland’s Federal Social Insurance Office disclosed errors in two formulas in August 2024 and published revised projections in September. The October Federal Council message gives a CHF 5.044 billion shortfall and fund cover of 60% in 2033 under the same policy scenario.

The figures are projections made in 2024. The annual shortfall is the difference between operating income and spending under the scenario with the 13th payment and a 20.2% federal contribution. The recalculation also updated forecast assumptions.

How the claim connects to the study

Blick’s 2033 shortfall and fund cover match the March 2024 federal funding-options table, as do its figures for alternative options. The corrected same-policy row appears in Table 3 of the October Federal Council message.
