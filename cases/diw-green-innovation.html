MajorGermany·Business & policy

A business article retains an innovation finding reversed by DIW’s correction

manage it · ap Verlag·Article published 6 June 2025 · still available

Corrected22 Jul 2025

Still says

Gleichzeitig gehen auch die Anmeldungen für Patente, die Verbrennungsmotoren sauberer machen, langfristig zurück.

Interview with Sonja Dobkowitz · effects on innovation investment · Open the source ↗

The article’s interview says greener consumer preferences cause applications for patents that make combustion engines cleaner to decline in the long term.

What changed

DIW corrected a calculation error. Its revised interview reports that applications for this same patent category rise in the long term.

The comparison concerns cleaner combustion technologies, called gray technologies in the study. DIW’s broader recommendation to consider consumer environmental preferences remains; the correction reverses this particular technology result.

Why it matters

The findings are presented to German business readers as evidence about corporate innovation and policy based on environmental product information.

How the claim connects to the study

The article names DIW researcher Sonja Dobkowitz and directly links the exact Wochenbericht PDF. Its reproduced interview says applications for cleaner-combustion patents decline in the long term. The corrected issue’s interview says these applications rise in the long term: the same category and time horizon.
