A prevention report’s press release still gives the original 9.3 million estimate

Medizinischer Dienst Bund · GKV-Spitzenverband

Why it matters

The figures describe the scale of statutory health insurance prevention programmes delivered in community settings such as schools, childcare and municipalities.

Still says

29 November 2023 · release still online
rund 9,3 Mio. Menschen in mehr als 50.000 Lebenswelten

Press release · health promotion in community settings · Open the source ↗

In EnglishThe joint press release says statutory health insurance prevention activities reached about 9.3 million people in community settings in 2022, a rise of 55% from 2021. Its report link leads to the corrected edition.

What changed

CorrectedSeptember 2024

The report’s September 2024 erratum fixes a calculation error. Estimated direct reach fell from 9,262,221 to 8,373,303, and the year-on-year increase from 55% to 40%.

The figures estimate how many people were reached by prevention activities in community settings. The count of settings is a separate measure.

How the claim connects to the study

The joint release names the 2023 prevention report and links the corrected second edition. The erratum on printed page 160 gives both versions of the count and its growth rate.
