A pesticide export motion cites a withdrawn global poisoning estimate

Die Linke · German political party · Bundestag motion 21/8073

Why it matters

The motion uses the older estimate to argue for restrictions on exports of unapproved pesticides and for action on residues in imported food.

Still says

21 September 2026
Weltweit kommt es jährlich zu rund 385 Millionen unbeabsichtigten akuten Pestizidvergiftungen, darunter etwa 11.000 Todesfälle.

Page 1 · grounds for the motion · Open the source ↗

In EnglishA motion by members of the Bundestag from Die Linke cites about 385 million unintentional acute pesticide poisonings worldwide each year as part of its case for export restrictions.

What changed

Retracted9 Oct 2024

The journal retracted the 2020 study over the way it used lifetime or unspecified time periods to estimate annual poisonings. The authors disputed the retraction. Their separate 2026 analysis estimated 402–433 million non-fatal cases per year across its sensitivity analyses.

Limits and context

The new study appeared on 2 September 2026, before the motion was filed. It still estimates a large global burden. Its fatal estimate of 11,413 also rounds to about 11,000. The global figures cover poisonings from many sources and do not quantify the share attributable to German exports.

How the claim connects to the study

The motion links to a 2023 Pesticide Action Network (PAN) Germany explainer that gives the same estimates and cites the exact 2020 paper in reference 1. The newer paper identifies the retracted predecessor and reports a new review with updated data and sensitivity analyses.

Boedeker, Watts, Clausing & Marquez · 2020 · BMC Public Health
The global distribution of acute unintentional pesticide poisoning: estimations based on a systematic review
10.1186/s12889-020-09939-0 ↗
